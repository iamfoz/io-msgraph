# Series exceptions written through instances

The live testing campaign of 2026-11-15 (.github cairn changes/live-testing-2026-11-15, reports/calendula/msgraph-microsoft.md M-F1 and part2-pimdir.md P2-F5) found a series with an EXDATE and an override landing on Graph as its master alone, through `calendula event create` and through `neverest sync -s msgraph-calendar`, the next sync then rewriting the store copy from the reduced series. The loss was in this crate's projection: `MsgraphEvent::from_ical` (src/v1/rest/users/events/ical.rs:210-215 before the change) reads the VEVENT without a RECURRENCE-ID alone, so `create_from_ical` and `update_from_ical` never saw an override, and `project` (ical.rs:557 before the change) consumes an EXDATE and keeps nothing of it. Graph takes both only through the instance, and no surface turned a series document into those instance writes. neverest has since pushed a series whole on its own side (776fca9, its change a-series-is-pushed-whole), reading the occurrences itself and patching each against the listed instance through `instance_update_from_ical`; calendula's direct create still writes the master alone.

Under `ical`, `MsgraphEventSeries::create_from_ical(contents)` and `update_from_ical(contents, base)` now cut a series document into `master`, the create body or update patch the `MsgraphEvent` constructors of the same name give, `instances`, one `MsgraphEventInstanceChange` per instance to change once the master is written, and `left_out`, the logical lines of what Graph cannot hold. A change carries `original_start` (Graph's `originalStart`: an RFC 3339 UTC instant, a date in an all-day series), the `window` `event_instances` lists it within (the day before, to two days after, stretched to where `base` moved its exception), `is_instance`, which matches a listed instance by its `originalStart` as an instant, and `MsgraphEventInstanceAction::Delete` (an EXDATE, or an override whose STATUS is CANCELLED) or `Update(Box<MsgraphEvent>)`, the patch of an override against the series carried to that occurrence, or against `base`'s exception on an update; an update also reverts an exception the document dropped. A master patch moving the series (start, end, all-day flag, recurrence) takes `base`'s exceptions and cancellations as dropped, the rule neverest encodes too, and changes the instances as after a create. `left_out` names RDATE and EXRULE lines, RECURRENCE-IDs with a RANGE, values in a zone neither table knows, exceptions beside a master that is no series, occurrences `base` cancels and the document restores, and an override's unmanaged lines other than its UID. `instance_update_from_ical` now shares the occurrence patch with the series projection. No coroutine was added: create, instances, update and delete already exist.

Decision recorded for the owner (proposal): a cancelled occurrence is carried by deleting its instance, not by the `cancel` action, since the delete is what reads back as an EXDATE and works on any event; on an organised meeting it sends the attendees a cancellation, as Outlook does. neverest made the same choice.

Tests written first and seen failing on a stub reproducing the old master-only projection (`instances` and `left_out` empty): `a_series_carries_its_exdate_and_override_to_their_instances` ("one patch and one delete: []"), `an_occurrence_cancelled_twice_or_by_its_status_is_deleted`, `an_update_writes_what_changed_of_the_instances`, `a_dropped_override_takes_the_series_back`, `an_override_moved_far_is_listed_where_it_stands`, `what_graph_cannot_hold_of_a_series_is_named` (left `[]`), `a_change_picks_its_instance_from_a_listing`, and in tests/coroutines.rs `a_series_reaches_its_instances_through_the_coroutines`, which creates the master, lists the instances on a scripted answer, and asserts the `PATCH /v1.0/me/events/I2` carrying the new start and end alone, then the `DELETE /v1.0/me/events/I3`. `an_update_moving_the_series_writes_its_instances_again` was written after the first implementation and failed on it (the held EXDATE named as restored) before the moved-series rule. All pass after the change, with the whole suite, clippy `-D warnings` and the no_std build. The live `ical_series_with_its_instances` (tests/msgraph.rs) was written and not run: this session had no account.
